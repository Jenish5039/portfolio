# Jeme Portfolio — Agent Guidelines & Creative Directive

This project follows the **Jeme-Specific Creative Directive** outlined in [`.agents/rules/jeme-creative-directive.md`](.agents/rules/jeme-creative-directive.md).

## Core Philosophy

1. **Authorship over spectacle**: `CONCEPT → STORY → ART DIRECTION → DETAIL` over `MORE ANIMATION → MORE 3D → MORE EFFECTS`.
2. **Positioning**: `DESIGN × TECHNOLOGY × AI × PRODUCT THINKING`.
3. **AI as an Accelerator**: Never frame Jeme as an "AI Designer", but as a *designer who leverages AI to accelerate exploration, prototyping, and high-fidelity frontend execution*.
4. **The Portfolio is a Product**: Rigorous IA, research-backed decisions, systemized design tokens, and performance.
5. **Case Studies First**: Focus on *Thinking*, *Research*, *Decisions*, *System*, *Execution*, and *Reflection*.
6. **Warm Obsidian Restraint**: Restraint + Precision + Hierarchy = Luxury.
7. **Intentional Typography**: Editorial (Cormorant/PT Serif), Interface (Inter/Space Grotesk), Technical (Geist Mono).
8. **Justified Interactions**: Every interaction must serve a distinct narrative and communicative purpose.

> **"Jeme thinks in systems, designs with intent, and builds what he imagines."**
